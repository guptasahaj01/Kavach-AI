TOPIC: Savings Account Minimum Average Balance (MAB) and Service Charges
CATEGORY: SAVINGS_MAB
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: Can an account balance turn negative due to penal charges?

ANSWER:
No. RBI guidelines expressly prohibit banks from turning savings account balances negative solely due to the levy of non-maintenance penal charges for failing to maintain minimum balance requirements.
