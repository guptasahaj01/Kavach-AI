REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What is the expected settlement time for RTGS recipient credit?

ANSWER:
Under RBI guidelines, the beneficiary bank must credit the funds to the recipient's account within 30 minutes of receiving the transfer message from the RBI RTGS central gateway.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
