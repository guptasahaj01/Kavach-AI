TOPIC: Immediate Payment Service (IMPS) Rules
CATEGORY: IMPS_IMMEDIATE
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What is the maximum daily per-transaction limit for IMPS?

ANSWER:
The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) revised the per-transaction limit for IMPS to Rs. 5,00,000 (increased from the previous Rs. 2,00,000 ceiling). Individual banks may specify lower limits based on risk assessment.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
