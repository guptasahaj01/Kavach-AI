TOPIC: Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) Protocols
CATEGORY: RTGS_TRANSACTIONS
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What details are required from the remitter to execute RTGS?

ANSWER:
The remitter must furnish the beneficiary name, bank name, branch name, beneficiary account number, and the recipient branch's 11-digit alphanumeric IFSC code.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
