TOPIC: Savings Account Minimum Average Balance (MAB) and Service Charges
CATEGORY: SAVINGS_MAB
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: Are banks required to notify customers prior to levying MAB penalties?

ANSWER:
Yes. Banks must notify customers via SMS, email, or letter when their balance falls below the stipulated threshold and provide a grace period of one month to replenish funds before applying penalties.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
