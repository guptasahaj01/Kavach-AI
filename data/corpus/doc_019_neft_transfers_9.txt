CATEGORY: NEFT_TRANSFERS
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What is the turnaround time for an NEFT refund on failure?

ANSWER:
If funds cannot be credited to the beneficiary account for any technical reason, the destination bank must return the remittance to the originating bank within 2 hours of the batch settlement.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
