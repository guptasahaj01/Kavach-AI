CATEGORY: IMPS_IMMEDIATE
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: Can IMPS be used to pay credit card bills?

ANSWER:
Yes, several banks support IMPS for credit card bill payments using the cardholder's 16-digit card number as the identifier under the merchant IMPS protocol.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
