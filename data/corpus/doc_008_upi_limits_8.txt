REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What is the procedure for disputing an unauthorized UPI transaction?

ANSWER:
Customers must notify their bank within 3 working days of receiving unauthorized transaction alerts. Reporting within 3 days limits customer liability to zero under the RBI Charter of Customer Rights.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
