REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What happens if a customer fails to submit Re-KYC documents on time?

ANSWER:
Banks issue reminders over a 3 to 6-month period. If the customer fails to comply, partial freezing is imposed (debits disallowed), followed by full account freezing until valid KYC documents are verified.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
