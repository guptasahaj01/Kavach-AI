REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What are the free ATM limits at other bank ATMs?

ANSWER:
Customers are entitled to 3 free transactions per month at other bank ATMs in 6 metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad) and 5 free transactions per month in non-metro locations.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
