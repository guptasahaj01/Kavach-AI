QUESTION: Is RTGS available on weekends and bank holidays?

ANSWER:
Yes, RTGS is operational round-the-clock (24x7x365) since December 2020, facilitating continuous interbank and customer gross fund transfers.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
