REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: Can IMPS transfers be initiated using MMID?

ANSWER:
Yes, IMPS allows remittance using a Mobile Money Identifier (MMID)—a 7-digit unique number linked with the customer's mobile number—without requiring the recipient's bank account number or IFSC.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
