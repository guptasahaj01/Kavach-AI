REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What security measures safeguard corporate RTGS gateways?

ANSWER:
Corporate RTGS utilizes Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), digital signature certificates (DSC Class 3), dual-authorization workflows (maker-checker controls), and IP-whitelisted VPN tunnels.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
