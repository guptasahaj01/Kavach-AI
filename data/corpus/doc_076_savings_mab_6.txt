TOPIC: Savings Account Minimum Average Balance (MAB) and Service Charges
CATEGORY: SAVINGS_MAB
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What are the free cash withdrawal limits at own-bank ATMs?

ANSWER:
Under RBI mandates, banks must provide account holders with a minimum of 5 free financial and non-financial transactions per month at their own-bank ATMs in all geographic locations.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
