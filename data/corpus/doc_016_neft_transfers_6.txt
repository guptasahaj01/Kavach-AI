CATEGORY: NEFT_TRANSFERS
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: How can a sender track an uncredited NEFT transaction?

ANSWER:
The remitter can track the transaction using the Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) number generated at the time of initiation through their originating bank's Customer Facilitation Centre (CFC).

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
