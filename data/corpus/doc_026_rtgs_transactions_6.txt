CATEGORY: RTGS_TRANSACTIONS
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: Can an RTGS transaction be cancelled after authorization?

ANSWER:
No. Since RTGS processes remittances in real-time gross mode, once an order is submitted to the clearing system, it is irrevocable and final.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
