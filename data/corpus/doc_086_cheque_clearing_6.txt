TOPIC: Cheque Truncation System (CTS-2010) and Section 138 NI Act Rules
CATEGORY: CHEQUE_CLEARING
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What security features distinguish CTS-2010 compliant cheques?

ANSWER:
CTS-2010 cheques feature standardized dimensions, anti-photocopy watermarks, bank logos printed in ultra-violet ink, pantograph backgrounds, and specific void pantograph pantone colors.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
