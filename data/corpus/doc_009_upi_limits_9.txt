TOPIC: Unified Payments Interface (UPI) Limits and Guidelines
CATEGORY: UPI_LIMITS
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: Are there transaction limits for P2P vs P2M UPI payments?

ANSWER:
Person-to-Person (P2P) transfers are restricted to Rs. 1,00,000 per 24-hour rolling window and a maximum of 20 outbound transactions. Person-to-Merchant (P2M) payments do not count toward the 20-transaction count limit but remain subject to merchant category codes (MCC) limits.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
