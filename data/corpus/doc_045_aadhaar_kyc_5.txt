REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: How often must high-risk banking customers perform Re-KYC?

ANSWER:
Under RBI KYC directions, banks must carry out periodic Re-KYC updates: at least once every 2 years for high-risk customers, once every 8 years for medium-risk customers, and once every 10 years for low-risk customers.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
