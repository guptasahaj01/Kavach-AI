TOPIC: Immediate Payment Service (IMPS) Rules
CATEGORY: IMPS_IMMEDIATE
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What precautions should be taken to prevent IMPS fraud?

ANSWER:
Customers should verify recipient details via penny-drop validation before transferring, never share MPIN or OTPs, and monitor SMS transaction alerts immediately.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
