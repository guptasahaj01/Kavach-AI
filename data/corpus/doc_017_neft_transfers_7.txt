TOPIC: National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) Rules
CATEGORY: NEFT_TRANSFERS
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: Can walk-in customers without an account initiate NEFT?

ANSWER:
Yes, walk-in customers can deposit cash at any NEFT-enabled branch for remittance up to Rs. 50,000 per transaction, subject to providing full name, address, and mobile number.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
