TOPIC: Savings Account Minimum Average Balance (MAB) and Service Charges
CATEGORY: SAVINGS_MAB
REGULATORY CONTEXT: Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Indian Banking Codes

QUESTION: What are typical MAB requirements across branch categories?

ANSWER:
Standard retail savings accounts generally require an MAB of Rs. 10,000 for Metro branches, Rs. 5,000 for Urban branches, Rs. 2,000 for Semi-Urban branches, and Rs. 1,000 for Rural branches.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: Handled according to standard retail customer banking protocols.
